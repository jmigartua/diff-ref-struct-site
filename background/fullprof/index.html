FullProf, the baseline

J. Rodríguez-Carvajal (ILL, formerly LLB); the FullProf Suite with WinPLOTR, EdPCR and FullProfAPP. The complete inventory of what it does for crystal-structure refinement from powder diffraction (log L013, brought up to date with the FullProf News pages 2001–2026 and the FullProfAPP manual in L017; "Since" gives the date a capability appeared after the 2001 manual). One row per capability: what it does, where it is set in the .pcr control file, and the sources in manuals/fullprof/ (path, page or line). That folder is kept in the private repository and not published, so the sources are named, not linked. GSAS-II and Jana2020 are read against this list for what FullProf lacks (L012).

Rodríguez-Carvajal, LLB Saclay; 139 pages, read page by page; the copy FullProf_Manual_1.pdf is byte-identical); FP_COMMANDS.pdf, the list of keywords for the COMMANDS block of a .pcr file (T. Roisnel, 2012–2023, 12 pages); the eleven tutorial folders: 1 WinPLOTR (FPSchool slides, ILL, December 2015, with a sequential example run in 2023), 2 Y2O3 structure solution (undated), 3 Co-doped YBCO (HERCULES course, undated), 4 spinel MgAl2O4 (text exercise, undated), 5 quantitative phase analysis (slides, undated, with papers of 2001 and 2002), 6 microstructure (paper of about 2002), 7 symmetry modes (May 2011), 8 rigid bodies (2002 files, later slides), 9 simulated annealing of organic compounds (outputs of 2017), 10 FullProfAPP (FullProf School, October 2023), 11 FAULTS (FullProf School, April 2025; FAULTS manual September 2018); the FP_tests folder of the FullProf developers (308 files, 130 .pcr, driven by test_fp.bat, listing of March 2025); the FullProf News pages (ILL, January 2001 to May 2026, 27 files in news/, cited by line of the raw .htm, dates as the News gives them); four documents in docs/: TOF_FullProf.pdf ("Dec. 2003 (Draft)", 11 pages), Microstructural_effects.pdf (LLB version of the microstructure paper, undated, PDF of November 2003, 11 pages), GLOpSAnn.pdf (October 2008, updated April 2020, 5 pages), Short_guide_to_MolPDF.pdf (November 2016, 6 pages); and the FullProfAPP manual v1.3.3 (9 June 2025, docs/fullprofapp, HTML cited by line). Not found in the material

  • Pawley fitting: the manual names Pawley's method only as a relative of profile matching (FullProf_Manual.pdf p. 14); FullProf implements Le Bail.
  • Surface-roughness corrections (Suortti, Pitschke and others) by name: only the Bragg–Brentano microabsorption P0/Cp/\(\tau\) correction, whose references [25], [26] are not listed in the 2001 manual (p. 55).
  • Thin-sample transparency shift: the manual gives the formula but says the correction "is not implemented in FullProf" (p. 56).
  • Texture as a spherical-harmonic orientation distribution: FullProf has the exponential and March–Dollase functions, multi-axial March–Dollase (Nor=2, up to five directions, 2003), its anvil-cell variant (Nor=3) and a per-reflection correction file for a known external texture (2004); the QPA paper used external pole figures (Quantitative-prefOrient.pdf).
  • Sabine-type powder extinction for constant-wavelength data: only one TOF powder parameter and a SHELX-type single-crystal parameter (pp. 63, 110).
  • Fundamental-parameters peak shapes (emission profile, slits, detector convolved from geometry): only the Finger–Cox–Jephcoat axial-divergence term.
  • hkl-dependent asymmetry: "No hkl-dependent asymmetry model is currently available" (p. 31).
  • Amorphous-phase modelling as a phase (other than Debye-like background terms or the internal-standard calculation).
  • General 2D image integration (image plates, area detectors): WinPLOTR-2006 only displays ILL single-crystal detector frames (D9, D10, D15, D19; News 13 August 2007, 24 June 2009), and Get_Data integrates only the ILL D2B detector (2023).
