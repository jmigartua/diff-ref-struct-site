FullProf

FullProf, the baseline

J. Rodríguez-Carvajal (ILL, formerly LLB); the FullProf Suite with WinPLOTR, EdPCR and FullProfAPP. The complete inventory of what it does for crystal-structure refinement from powder diffraction (log L013). One row per capability: what it does, where it is set in the .pcr control file, and the sources in manuals/fullprof/ (path, page or line). That folder is kept in the private repository and not published, so the sources are named, not linked. GSAS-II and Jana2020 are read against this list for what FullProf lacks (L012).

Sources read. The FullProf manual "An introduction to the program FullProf", version July 2001 for FullProf.2k (J. Rodríguez-Carvajal, LLB Saclay; 139 pages, read page by page; the copy FullProf_Manual_1.pdf is byte-identical); FP_COMMANDS.pdf, the list of keywords for the COMMANDS block of a .pcr file (T. Roisnel, 2012–2023, 12 pages); the eleven tutorial folders: 1 WinPLOTR (FPSchool slides, ILL, December 2015, with a sequential example run in 2023), 2 Y2O3 structure solution (undated), 3 Co-doped YBCO (HERCULES course, undated), 4 spinel MgAl2O4 (text exercise, undated), 5 quantitative phase analysis (slides, undated, with papers of 2001 and 2002), 6 microstructure (paper of about 2002), 7 symmetry modes (May 2011), 8 rigid bodies (2002 files, later slides), 9 simulated annealing of organic compounds (outputs of 2017), 10 FullProfAPP (FullProf School, October 2023), 11 FAULTS (FullProf School, April 2025; FAULTS manual September 2018); and the FP_tests folder of the FullProf developers (308 files, 130 .pcr, driven by test_fp.bat, listing of March 2025). All of it lies in manuals/fullprof/, which is not published. Every citation below was checked against the page or line it names. "Not in the 2001 manual" marks capabilities seen only in the later material.

Kept for later

  • Magnetic structures. Commensurate and incommensurate magnetic structures: moments in the magnetic cell or by propagation vectors (up to 24), Fourier components, helices, cones, basis functions of irreps, Shubnikov groups, magnetic R-factor.   • Modulated structures and superspace. Commensurate displacement modulations (Jbt=\(\pm\)15) in the 2001 manual; magnetic superspace groups (Jbt=\(\pm\)7) in the tests. FullProf_Manual.pdf, pp. 106–107 · DyMnGe_SuperSpace.pcr L72

  • Structure solution. Space-group search (Check_Group), Monte Carlo and simulated annealing on integrated intensities or profile points, with rigid bodies and symmetry modes; Fourier and Patterson maps via GFourier. FullProf_Manual.pdf, pp. 126–129 · Tutorial-Y2O3.pdf, pp. 8–9 · pb_san.pcr L66–67

Out of scope

  • Single-crystal data. Integrated intensities of single crystals (F\(^{2}\) or F, twins with up to 6 hkl per observation, domain scales, extinction, λ/2), alone or with powder data. FullProf_Manual.pdf, p. 5 · FullProf_Manual.pdf, pp. 62–63 · FullProf_Manual.pdf, pp. 138–139 · prnio.pcr L1–5
  • Polarised-neutron flipping ratios. Single-crystal flipping-ratio refinement with a multipole form factor, seen only in the tests. 100K_c_vert-fr0.pcr L29–32 · 100K-c_vert.int L3
  • Electron diffraction. Only Mol_tpcr's JOB 4 (electrons) for writing simulated-annealing .pcr files; no 3D electron-diffraction refinement is documented. FullProf-Tutorial-RigidBody.pdf, p. 19

Not found in the material

  • Pawley fitting: the manual names Pawley's method only as a relative of profile matching (FullProf_Manual.pdf p. 14); FullProf implements Le Bail.
  • Surface-roughness corrections (Suortti, Pitschke and others) by name: only the Bragg–Brentano microabsorption P0/Cp/\(\tau\) correction, whose references [25], [26] are not listed in the 2001 manual (p. 55).
  • Thin-sample transparency shift: the manual gives the formula but says the correction "is not implemented in FullProf" (p. 56).
  • Texture beyond the exponential and March–Dollase functions (no spherical-harmonic orientation distribution); the QPA paper used external pole figures (Quantitative-prefOrient.pdf).
  • Sabine-type powder extinction for constant-wavelength data: only one TOF powder parameter and a SHELX-type single-crystal parameter (pp. 63, 110).
  • Fundamental-parameters peak shapes (emission profile, slits, detector convolved from geometry): only the Finger–Cox–Jephcoat axial-divergence term.
  • hkl-dependent asymmetry: "No hkl-dependent asymmetry model is currently available" (p. 31).
  • Amorphous-phase modelling as a phase (other than Debye-like background terms or the internal-standard calculation).
  • 2D image integration: no image-plate or area-detector reduction appears in the material.
  • Formulas and parameters for the TOF Ikeda–Carpenter profile (Npr=13) beyond its name.
