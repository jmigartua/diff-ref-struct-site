I042026-10-16, Josudiscussed→ core

Inference for magnetic structures, with MAGNDATA as the family and the test bed

A derivative of the Bayesian inference before refinement (L286): extend the approach to the analysis of magnetic structures, with their symmetry treated thoroughly in the superspace formalism. The Bilbao Crystallographic Server's magnetic structures database could be a massive source of data for it.

2026-10-16discussion Assessed with MAGNDATA's pages and the literature: the database holds models, not patterns, so it is the measured prior and the test bed, not data to refine; 91 % single-irrep in a 2026 analysis; no program compares magnetic candidates by evidence; the core has no magnetic scattering yet, which is the real cost.

I052026-10-16, Josuworkshop→ core

Maximum entropy: what became of it, and what of it belongs in the inference

Some twenty-five years ago the maximum entropy method was a promising approach to solving and refining crystal structures. Did it succeed, how far was it adopted or abandoned, was it really for refinement; could it be resurrected, or derived into something like the Bayesian inference and the exploration of configuration space?

2026-10-21discussion Audited with I06 (its workshop note of 21 October, §4): nothing found contradicts it; the entropic refinement still has no precedent for powders; WPEM (2026) is the nearest different estimator to compare with. Its references, until now only in the log's sources, are on this page with how each was checked. Josu proposes a project of its own for the topic (I07); this page keeps the record and points there.

I062026-10-19, Josuworkshop→ core

The minimisation itself: what molecular dynamics, DFT relaxations, Monte Carlo and machine learning can lend to the Rietveld engine, with the same results

Beyond reproducing FullProf and GSAS-II, innovate in the minimisation, not only in the information it uses (as mode crystallography does). The Rietveld method is fixed; is its least-squares minimisation the best one can do, in reliability and in speed? Molecular dynamics and DFT codes (SIESTA, for one) relax structures into energy minima with algorithms of their own; Monte Carlo might approach the minimum of a many-parameter problem faster. The application differs, but think about what could be borrowed, and propose another possible way of refining that still lands on the very same results. And, asked straight after: machine learning, neural networks, physically informed models?

2026-10-21discussion Audited with I05 at Josu's request, with the Bayesian-optimisation primer he sent: Bayesian optimisation belongs where a whole refinement is the black box (BBO-Rietveld 2020, the Agada–Biswas papers of 2025–26, Spotlight 2025), never as the minimiser; twelve precedents the first assessment missed, by section (Antoniadis 1990, David 2004 twice, Gagin–Levin 2015, Coelho 2005, Toby 2024, Kerman 2012, Segal 2025, AiMat-DRX, the agents, WPEM, HMC in scattering, optimal transport in diffraction); three more path methods (an optimal-transport misfit for capture, the Poisson likelihood, a parameter released by its derivative); the references on the page; a project of its own proposed as I07. Second workshop note.

Perhaps it is time to move the content of this topic, the ideas I05 and I06, the sessions they came out of and the audit of 21 October, to another website dedicated to it, to go deeper: all the references, what each of them claims, and how all of them could be taken into account, in a separate, independent project, first for deepening and only after for implementing, if possible and if it makes sense, in the app we are working on.

The scaffold made locally the same day, not published; the name, the repositories and the first readings are Josu's to decide.

An interactive map of a series, with the height clipped on the colour bar, to find the phase transitions

While reading the maps of the ESRF ID22 series of sample 400_4 (the esrf-datuak-irene project, 23 October 2026): a kind of map, but interactive, so that the exploration of the map is made easier for finding the phase transitions. It should have the option to select in the height, done on the colour-map legend at the right: the idea is to be able, so to say, to get rid of the big-height peaks and "go down" to the details.

2026-10-23said Said again by Josu, the features not finished: a zoom in the temperature against 2\(\theta\) plane, to focus on the region wanted; and a window opened from the CLI without the app, the browser or a better option. Assessed: zoom with the clip following it and regions saved in the set file; one viewer component hosted by the app, by a self-contained browser page written by drs, and later by a window of the core's own.

    What this is

    The working site of one project: to build a Mac app for Rietveld refinement of powder diffraction data, after a study of the programs the community uses for it. It holds the brief, that study, the design, a dated log of every step, the release notes and the manuals of the core and the app.
