Parameters: the theory

Parameters: the theory

The parameter dictionary (L044, L046): every parameter the core reads or will refine, under a readable name, with the equation it enters and where that equation comes from. The core's names, the app's help and this page are generated from the same file, so they cannot drift apart.

The papers' own equations have not yet been read against these: the session that wrote the dictionary could not reach the journals, so each equation is cited to the page where FullProf's manual or the microstructure paper prints it, and each paper's reference was checked against bibliographic records only. Where FullProf computes something other than its manual prints, the core's default follows the manual and the FullProf convention keeps FullProf's rule (L046).

45 entries, 39 refinable. Each gives the parameter's name in the core, its symbol, unit and meaning, the equation it enters with the paper behind it and the page where the equation is printed, its FullProf and GSAS-II equivalents, the range measured on the 240 .pcr files of the FullProf material that the core reads, the default, and when to refine it (after FullProf's manual, p. 14). Generated from data/parameters.yml; do not edit by hand.
