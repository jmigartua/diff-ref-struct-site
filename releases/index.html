Structure factors with FullProf's conventions

The last item of phase 1 (L030): structure factors for X-rays, with anomalous terms, and for neutrons, with FullProf's occupancies and displacement parameters. They are checked against every F\(^{2}\) FullProf printed in the material (12026 reflections), against gemmi for the four reference structures, and by symmetry properties in all 530 settings.

Added

  • Structure factors summed over all the operators of the group, with FullProf's Occ (chemical occupancy × m/M), Biso and betas; conversions between Occ and chemical occupancy, and from U to betas and B. |F|² is FullProf's printed F\(^{2}\): f′ and f″ included, the overall B left out.
  • Scattering tables generated from CrysFML (FullProf's library): 214 X-ray form factors, ions included; f′ and f″ for 98 elements at the Kα1 of Cr, Fe, Cu, Mo and Ag, taken at the anode nearest to λ as FullProf takes them; 108 neutron scattering lengths.
  • A small CIF reader: cell; symmetry operators, Hall or H-M symbol; atom sites; U or B; anisotropic U_ij or B_ij; ionic type symbols.
  • drs sf <file.cif | symbol + cell + --atom …> --dmin <d> [--xray <λ or anode> | --neutron]: h k l, multiplicity, d, |F|, F\(^{2}\).
  • SpaceGroup::from_fullprof_symbol: a symbol read as FullProf reads it, e.g. "F d -3 m" as origin choice 2.
  • Test fullprof_structure_factors: PPH3, citric acid (X-rays) and LaMnO3 (neutrons, special positions). Of 12026 printed F\(^{2}\), all agree within the precision of the printed atoms, at most 0.37 of that bound. Controls without f′, f″ or with chemical occupancies fail.
  • Test gemmi_structure_factors: 10 phases of the four reference structures (Y2O3, MgAl2O4, Co-doped YBCO, the QPA phases), 1435 reflections, X-rays and neutrons. With gemmi's coefficients the agreement is 6·\(10^{-8}\); table by table, only O\(^{2-}\) differs.
  • Test structure_factor_properties (530 settings, 182320 reflections): absences, Friedel and Bijvoet pairs, real F with −1 at the origin, equivalent reflections, and the occupancy convention against the structure written out in P 1. Test cif_structures.

Known limitations

  • The material holds no FullProf structure factors for the four reference structures. They were checked against gemmi; FullProf's own (Hkl = −3) can be produced by running FullProf on their tutorial files.
  • The O\(^{2-}\) X-ray form factor of CrysFML (two Gaussians) differs from gemmi's (four); the sources of each were not checked.
  • f′ and f″ come from five tabulated anodes only, as in FullProf; values at other energies (synchrotron) cannot be given yet.
  • The CIF reader takes the first block with atom sites, skips text fields and ignores standard uncertainties.

Reflections as FullProf lists them

The fourth item of phase 1 (L028): the unique reflections of a phase between two d-spacings or in a 2\(\theta\) range, with systematic absences derived from the operators, FullProf's choice of representative, multiplicities, d and 2\(\theta\), checked against 14337 reflections printed by FullProf and against direct structure factors in all 530 settings.
